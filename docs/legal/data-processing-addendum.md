# ProFixIQ Data Processing Addendum (DPA) (DRAFT — attorney review required before use)

**Last updated:** [date]

This Data Processing Addendum ("**DPA**") supplements the ProFixIQ
Terms of Service between **[Legal Entity Name]** ("ProFixIQ,"
"**Processor**") and the Shop ("**Controller**") and applies to
personal data ProFixIQ processes on the Shop's behalf and instructions
in the course of providing the Service, including data about the
Shop's customers, employees, and contractors.

## 1. Roles

1.1 For personal data the Shop submits about its own customers and
employees ("**Customer Personal Data**"), the Shop is the Controller
(or "Business" under CCPA) and ProFixIQ is the Processor (or "Service
Provider").

1.2 For data ProFixIQ collects about the Shop's account and billing
relationship with ProFixIQ, ProFixIQ acts as an independent Controller,
governed by the Privacy Policy rather than this DPA.

## 2. Processing Instructions

2.1 ProFixIQ will process Customer Personal Data only to provide,
secure, and support the Service in accordance with the Shop's
documented instructions (which include the Shop's configuration
choices within the Service) and this DPA, unless otherwise required by
law, in which case ProFixIQ will inform the Shop unless prohibited from
doing so.

2.2 ProFixIQ will not sell Customer Personal Data or use it for any
purpose other than providing the Service, except as necessary to
operate AI features the Shop has enabled (see the AI & Voice Data
Policy) or as aggregated/de-identified data that cannot reasonably
identify an individual.

## 3. Confidentiality and Personnel

ProFixIQ ensures that personnel authorized to process Customer Personal
Data are subject to confidentiality obligations and receive appropriate
training, and access is limited to what each role requires
(least-privilege).

## 4. Subprocessors

4.1 The Shop authorizes ProFixIQ to engage the subprocessors listed in
the Subprocessor List, incorporated by reference.

4.2 ProFixIQ will impose data-protection obligations on subprocessors
materially equivalent to those in this DPA and remains responsible for
subprocessor performance.

4.3 ProFixIQ will provide notice of a new subprocessor at least [14 /
30] days before it begins processing Customer Personal Data, and the
Shop may object on reasonable data-protection grounds by [contacting
ProFixIQ within that window]; if unresolved, the Shop may terminate the
affected Service component.

## 5. Security Measures

ProFixIQ implements technical and organizational measures appropriate
to the risk, including: encryption of data in transit; database-level
tenant isolation (row-level security policies scoped to shop
membership); least-privilege access to production systems; and
monitoring/alerting for anomalous access. Details are in the Security
Policy.

## 6. Data Subject Requests

Where an individual submits a request (access, correction, deletion,
export) directly to ProFixIQ concerning Customer Personal Data, ProFixIQ
will promptly forward the request to the relevant Shop and provide
reasonable assistance to help the Shop respond, unless legally
prohibited from doing so.

## 7. Personal Data Breach Notification

ProFixIQ will notify the Shop without undue delay, and in any event
within [72] hours of becoming aware, of a confirmed breach of security
leading to accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration,
unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data,
consistent with the Incident Response Plan, and will provide
information reasonably available to help the Shop meet its own
notification obligations.

## 8. Deletion and Return of Data

On termination of the Service (or earlier on request), ProFixIQ will,
at the Shop's choice, make Customer Personal Data available for export
and delete or anonymize it within the periods set out in the Data
Retention & Deletion Policy, except where retention is required by law.

## 9. Audits and Records

ProFixIQ will make available information reasonably necessary to
demonstrate compliance with this DPA (e.g., summaries of relevant
security certifications or audit results, once available) and will
allow, at the Shop's expense and on reasonable notice, an audit limited
to compliance with this DPA, subject to confidentiality protections and
not more than once per 12-month period absent a substantiated incident.

## 10. International Transfers

Where Customer Personal Data originating outside the United States is
transferred to the United States or another country, the parties will
rely on an appropriate transfer mechanism (e.g., Standard Contractual
Clauses) as required by applicable law. [To be finalized by counsel
based on the Shop's jurisdictions.]

## 11. Liability

Liability under this DPA is subject to the limitation of liability in
the Terms of Service, except where applicable law prohibits limiting
liability for data protection violations.
