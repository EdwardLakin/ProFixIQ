# ProFixIQ Acceptable Use Policy (DRAFT — attorney review required before use)

**Last updated:** [date]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") applies to every Shop and
Authorized User of the ProFixIQ Service and is incorporated into the
Terms of Service. Violations may result in content/account restriction,
suspension, or termination as described in the Terms.

## 1. No unauthorized access or security testing

Do not attempt to access another Shop's data, probe, scan, or test the
vulnerability of the Service or its infrastructure, bypass rate limits
or authentication, or use credentials you are not authorized to use.
Report suspected vulnerabilities per our Responsible Disclosure Policy
instead of exploiting them.

## 2. No misuse of AI features

Do not use AI-assisted suggestion or voice-transcription features to
generate deceptive estimates, misrepresent diagnostic findings to
customers, or present AI output as a certified inspection or licensed
professional's opinion where certification or licensure is legally
required. AI output must be reviewed by a qualified technician or
advisor before being relied upon or billed.

## 3. No unlawful or harmful use

Do not use the Service to violate any applicable law, including
consumer-protection, data-protection, wage-and-hour/payroll, or
motor-vehicle-repair regulations, or to store or transmit content that
is unlawful, harassing, defamatory, or infringing.

## 4. No abuse of shared infrastructure

Do not send spam or unsolicited bulk communications through
portal/notification features, attempt to disrupt the Service for other
tenants (e.g., excessive automated requests, denial-of-service-style
traffic), or introduce malware.

## 5. Data accuracy and consent

Ensure you have the necessary rights and, where applicable, customer or
employee consent to enter personal data (including photos, signatures,
and vehicle information) into the Service, and that data you enter is
accurate to the best of your knowledge.

## 6. Payment and billing integrity

Do not use the Service's billing/invoicing features to defraud
customers or evade tax or consumer-protection obligations.

## 7. Reporting violations

Report suspected violations of this AUP to [abuse contact email].
ProFixIQ may investigate and take action, including immediate
suspension, where necessary to prevent harm to the Service, other
tenants, or third parties.
