# ProFixIQ Privacy Policy (DRAFT — attorney review required before use)

**Last updated:** [date]

This Privacy Policy explains how **[Legal Entity Name]** ("ProFixIQ,"
"we," "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal
information through the ProFixIQ platform (the "Service"). It applies
to Shops, their Authorized Users, and, where the Shop's own customers
interact with ProFixIQ-hosted portals (booking, invoices, inspection
sign-off), those customers as well.

For most personal data about a Shop's own customers and employees,
**the Shop is the data controller and ProFixIQ is a data processor**
acting on the Shop's instructions — see the Data Processing Addendum.
This Policy also covers the data ProFixIQ collects as a controller in
its own right (e.g., Shop account/billing data, product usage
analytics).

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account & billing data:** Shop name, business address, owner/admin
contact details, subscription plan, and billing metadata (Stripe
handles and stores actual payment card details; ProFixIQ receives
tokens/metadata only, not full card numbers).

**Operational data entered by Shops:** customer names and contact
information, vehicle identification (VIN, plate, make/model/year),
work order and inspection records, estimates and invoices, parts and
inventory records, employee/technician records including payroll and
timeclock data, scheduling data, and photos/signatures captured during
inspections or work authorization.

**Fleet and property management data:** unit/vehicle records, driver
records, pre-trip inspection and defect data, property/unit records,
and maintenance requests, where the Shop uses those modules.

**AI feature data:** text prompts and vehicle/work-order context sent
to AI suggestion features; audio streamed to a voice-transcription
feature (see the AI & Voice Data Policy for how this is handled and
what is retained).

**Automatically collected data:** device/browser metadata, IP address,
authentication logs, and product usage/error telemetry used to secure
and improve the Service.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to: provide and operate the Service; authenticate
users and enforce account/tenant boundaries; process subscription
billing through Stripe; provide AI-assisted suggestions and voice
transcription; send transactional communications (booking
confirmations, invoices, portal notifications); detect, investigate,
and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; and improve the
Service, including via aggregated or de-identified analysis.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Shop customer or
employee data to train third-party foundation models beyond what is
necessary to provide the AI features the Shop has enabled, and any such
use is described in the AI & Voice Data Policy.

## 3. How We Share Information

We share information with:

- **Subprocessors** who help us operate the Service — see the
  Subprocessor List (currently: Supabase for database/authentication/
  file storage, Vercel for application hosting, Stripe for payment
  processing, and OpenAI for AI suggestion and voice-transcription
  features).
- **The Shop's own personnel**, according to the roles and permissions
  the Shop configures.
- **Law enforcement or regulators**, where required by valid legal
  process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of ProFixIQ,
  our users, or the public.
- **A successor entity**, in connection with a merger, acquisition, or
  asset sale, subject to this Policy (or a materially similar one)
  continuing to apply.

## 4. Cross-Border Data Transfers

ProFixIQ's primary database infrastructure is hosted in the United
States (AWS us-east-2). If you or your customers are located outside
the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored,
and processed in the United States and other countries where our
subprocessors operate. Where required, we rely on appropriate
safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) for these transfers.
[Confirm mechanism with counsel for EU/UK/Canadian shops.]

## 5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as the Shop's account is
active and as needed to provide the Service, plus a limited period
after termination to allow data export and to meet legal, accounting,
or dispute-resolution obligations. See the Data Retention & Deletion
Policy for retention periods by data category.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct,
export, or delete your personal information, or to object to or
restrict certain processing. Where ProFixIQ acts as a processor for a
Shop's customer or employee data, requests should generally be directed
to the Shop; ProFixIQ will assist the Shop in responding as described
in the DPA. Where ProFixIQ acts as a controller (e.g., for Shop
account/billing data), requests can be sent to [privacy contact email].

## 7. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to
protect personal information, including encryption in transit,
tenant-isolation controls (row-level security) in our database,
least-privilege access controls, and monitoring. No system is
completely secure; see our Security Policy and Incident Response Plan
for how we handle and disclose incidents.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We will post updates here and, for material changes, provide additional
notice (e.g., email or in-product notice) with a reasonable transition
period.
